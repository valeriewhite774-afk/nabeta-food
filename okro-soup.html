Common Questions

Frequently Asked About Okro Soup

How do you achieve the perfect draw in your Okro Soup?
Fresh, finely cut okro cooked at the right temperature for just the right amount of time. The result is a perfectly silky draw that clings to every swallow without being slimy.
Does your Okro Soup contain ogiri?
Yes. We use a small amount of traditional ogiri (fermented locust beans) to deepen the umami flavour of the soup.
What is the best swallow for Okro Soup?
Eba is our default. The firmness of Eba is perfect for drawing through the silky Okro Soup.
