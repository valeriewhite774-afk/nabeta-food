About NabetaFood

Our Story

Real Delta Food. Cooked Fresh. Delivered Fast.

We are NabetaFood, a Warri-based food delivery brand built on one belief: authentic Nigerian food should arrive at your door hot, fresh, and exactly as it was made.

Who We Are

About NabetaFood

NabetaFood is a food delivery brand born in Warri, Delta State. We specialise in authentic Nigerian soups cooked fresh per order and delivered in 15 - 30 minutes.

Behind every bowl is Chef Mercy Mone, a professional cook with over 40 years of experience, including years as a contract chef at the WRPC Refinery and hundreds of catering events across Delta State.

What Drives Us

Our Mission

"To bring the authentic taste of Delta cuisine to every door in Warri. Cooked properly, priced fairly, and delivered fast."

The Origin

Why NabetaFood Was Started

Warri takes its food seriously. Yet finding a genuinely good bowl of soup without leaving your house was harder than it should have been.

NabetaFood was started to fix that. To make it possible for anyone in Warri to eat something truly worth eating without leaving home.
