Frequently Asked About Vegetable Soup

What type of vegetables do you use?
We use a blend of fresh Efo Riro leaves, Ugu (pumpkin leaves), and seasonal local greens, all hand-plucked and prepared fresh on the day of cooking.
Is the Vegetable Soup suitable for lighter eaters?
Yes. It is one of our lighter options nutritionally, packed with vitamins and natural fibre. Ideal if you want a flavourful but less heavy meal.
What swallow goes with Vegetable Soup?
We recommend Fufu as the default. The soft, smooth consistency complements the textured, leafy nature of the soup perfectly.
