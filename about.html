Who We Are

About NabetaFood

NabetaFood is a food delivery brand born in Warri, Delta State. We specialise in authentic Nigerian soups cooked fresh per order and delivered to your door in 15 - 30 minutes.

Behind every bowl is Chef Mercy Mone, a professional cook with over 40 years of experience, including years as a contract chef at the WRPC Refinery and hundreds of catering events across Delta State. The food you receive is not reheated. It is not batch-cooked. It is made for you, when you order it.

Our Mission

The Origin

Why NabetaFood Was Started

Warri takes its food seriously. Yet finding a genuinely good bowl of soup without leaving your house was harder than it should have been. Delivery options either served generic food or reheated meals that had been sitting for hours.

NabetaFood was started to fix that. To build something that respected the food, respected the customer, and made it possible for anyone in Warri to eat something truly worth eating without leaving home.
